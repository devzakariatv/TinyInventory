# Privacy Policy for Tiny Inventory Shelf

**Effective date:** October 6, 2026  
**App name:** Tiny Inventory Shelf  
**Package name / bundle ID:** com.qelvyn.tinyinventoryr6p4  
**Applies to:** Google Play and the Apple App Store

This is the same policy for both stores. Tiny Inventory Shelf handles information the same way on Android and iOS.

## Summary

Tiny Inventory Shelf stores your collection on your device and does not send it to us. We do not collect, sell, or share personal information. There is no account.

## Information we collect

We do not collect personal information.

We do not operate an account system, analytics service, advertising network, or cloud database for this app. The developer does not receive your collection, your notes, or a copy of what you place on the clipboard.

## Information stored on your device

When you add an item, the app saves it locally on that device so it is still there the next time you open Tiny Inventory Shelf. A saved item can include:

- a name
- an emoji you pick
- a category
- a quantity
- an optional note
- the time it was created and last updated

That information stays in local app storage (shared preferences on the device). It is not uploaded. Uninstalling the app, or deleting an item inside the app, removes it from the device. We cannot restore a collection after it is deleted, because we never receive a copy.

A new installation starts empty. The app does not ship with sample items.

## Clipboard

The Copy buttons place text you choose — one item, the collection, or a statistics summary — onto the device clipboard. The app does this only after you tap Copy. We do not read the clipboard, and we do not transmit clipboard contents.

## What we do not do

- We do not ask you to create an account.
- We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or location.
- We do not show ads.
- We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.
- We do not sell or share data with third parties.
- We do not use the collection for advertising or profiling.

## Permissions

Tiny Inventory Shelf does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo access. Copy uses the system clipboard, which does not require a special permission prompt.

## Children’s privacy

Tiny Inventory Shelf is a general-purpose collection tracker. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app does not send data to us, there is no personal information for us to delete from a server. A parent or guardian can remove local data by deleting items in the app or by uninstalling it.

## Data retention and deletion

You control the shelf. Edit or delete any item inside the app. Uninstalling Tiny Inventory Shelf removes the locally stored collection from that device. We do not keep a server copy.

## Security

Collection data stays in the app’s private local storage, protected by the device operating system. You are responsible for the security of the device itself, including its lock screen.

## International users

Because the collection is stored only on your device, we do not transfer it to another country.

## Store disclosures

### Google Play Data safety

- Data collected: No
- Data shared: No
- Data sold: No
- Account required: No
- Data is stored only on the device by the app, for the app’s own features
- Clipboard copy is started by the user and is not collected by the developer

### Apple App Privacy

- Data Not Collected
- No tracking
- No data used to track you
- No data linked to you

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted with a new effective date and included with the app listing. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

## Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the developer contact listed on the Google Play store listing and the Apple App Store listing for Tiny Inventory Shelf. Please include “Tiny Inventory Shelf privacy” in the subject so the request is easy to find.

We do not operate a separate account or data portal, because the app does not hold your collection on a server.
